---------------------------------------------------------------------
OPTIONAL SIDE PANEL: WHAT IT SAVES (SIMULATED)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Panel title: In the 30-day digital twin (simulated)

CHAKRA at tau 0.1 vs a fixed route that visits every bin daily:
  - 28% fewer km            (205.7 vs 286.8 km)
  - 62% less route time     (2,159 vs 5,647 minutes)
  - 70% fewer pickups       (445 vs 1,500)

CHAKRA at tau 0.1 vs a simple "visit when > 70% full" rule:
  - 47% fewer overflow events (18.7 vs 35.3) for slightly fewer km
    (205.7 vs 208.6 km)

Panel footnote: Simulated campus, mean of 3 seeds; not field measurements.
Trade-off: the fixed daily route has 0 overflows but drives the most.
